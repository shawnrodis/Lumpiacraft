Chef's Practical Tips

  • • Garlic & Carrots: Since head sizes vary, aim for about 2 whole heads of garlic for every ~1.3 lbs meat.
  • • Egg Wash: 1 egg wash usually suffices for up to 2–3 lbs of meat. Add a splash of water if it runs low.
  • • Magic Sarap & Seasoning: 1 pack is approx. 8g (1.5 tsp). Adjust to taste.
  • • Cornstarch: Acts as a binder. If the mixture feels wet, add an extra tablespoon.
